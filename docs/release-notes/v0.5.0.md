# Bit Browser 0.5.0: the forever-browser update

The biggest update yet. Everything here works on the phone and the computer unless it says otherwise.

## Never lose anything
- **Import from Chrome, Brave or Edge:** bookmarks and passwords (export them to a file, then Settings → Tools → Import). The computer finds Chrome, Brave and Edge on its own and can bring in bookmarks and history too.
- **Backup and restore:** one encrypted file with your tabs, bookmarks, passwords, 2FA codes, notes, settings and XP. Save it to Google Drive or a folder, and restore it on a new phone. Only your passphrase can open it.
- **One-tap updates (Android):** when a new version is out, tap UPDATE in the banner and it downloads and installs.
- **2FA codes:** Bit Browser can be your authenticator. Scan a site's QR code, see the 6-digit codes, and fill them in when a site asks. They sync only if you turn on password sync.
- **Password check:** finds weak and reused passwords, and (if you turn it on) passwords that appeared in data breaches. Only the first 5 characters of a scrambled version ever leave your device.
- **Passkeys:** turned on where Android allows it. Some sites will still ask for a password.

## Built around your work
- **Package tracker:** Bit Browser spots UPS, USPS, FedEx, Canada Post, DHL, Purolator and Amazon tracking numbers on any page and offers to TRACK them. They're kept in Packages (Menu), where you can open the carrier's page or ask Bitter where a package is.
- **Label printing presets:** choose 4×6 or letter and your printer once (Print → printer chip in the PDF viewer). Auto-print uses it too.
- **Ask Bitter about a PDF:** open a PDF (like an insurance policy) and tap ASK BITTER to get a summary and ask questions.
- **Notes:** highlight text and pick "Save to notes", or save the whole page. Notes are kept per Space and sync.
- **Read later:** save an article and read it offline later from your Reading list.

## Getting around faster
- **Command bar:** type `>` in the address bar (Ctrl+K on the computer) to jump to any tab, bookmark, page you've visited, setting or action.
- **Tab groups** with colors inside each Space.
- **Swipe the bottom bar** left or right to switch tabs, or up to see all tabs (phone).
- **Voice search:** tap the search icon next to the address bar and speak (phone).
- **Split screen and vertical tabs** (computer): two pages side by side.

## Video and media
- **Keeps playing in the background:** videos and podcasts keep going with the screen off, with play/pause in the notification (phone).
- **Picture-in-picture:** a full-screen video keeps playing in a small window when you leave the app (phone).
- **Speed control** (0.5×–3×, remembered per site), a **sleep timer**, and **save images and videos** (direct files only; not YouTube or other streaming sites). Find them under Menu → Media.

## Bitter, smarter
- **Watched pages and price tracking:** Menu → Watch this page tells you when it changes or the price drops.
- **Search your history in plain words**, like "that shipping supplies site last week".
- **AI on your computer:** use Ollama or LM Studio over Wi-Fi with no AI key (Settings → AI on my computer).

## Privacy as strong as Brave
- **HTTPS upgrade:** secure versions of sites are opened automatically.
- **Cookie banners are rejected for you.**
- **Fingerprinting protection:** stops sites from recognizing your device from small details.
- **Tor:** route everything through Tor with the free Orbot app (phone), or private windows over Tor when Tor is running (computer).
- **Hide my email:** create email aliases with SimpleLogin or addy.io (your own account).

## 128bit family
- **Kids mode:** PIN, safe search, allowed or blocked sites, and a daily time limit.
- **Family sharing:** share bookmarks or passwords with family through an encrypted shared code (needs sync set up).
- **128bit account:** the sign-in screen is ready and shows "coming soon" until the 128bit server exists.

## Good to know
- Nothing here has been tested on every phone yet. If something doesn't work, send a screenshot.
- Voice search isn't available on the computer yet.
- Tor needs the Orbot app on Android. It covers all tabs while it's on, not just private ones.
