# Bit Browser 0.6.2

## Calls go to your phone app
- Tapping **Call** or a phone number on a page (like a store on Google Maps) now opens your phone app with the number already filled in. You press call yourself.
- Want BitCaller instead? Settings → 128bit family → **Call phone numbers with BitCaller**.

## A fresh homepage every time you open Bit Browser
- Bit Browser now opens on a new homepage each time. Your tabs from last time, including pinned tabs, are still there in **Tabs**.
- Prefer to land on your last tab? Settings → Tabs → turn off **Start on a new homepage**.

| You have | Download |
