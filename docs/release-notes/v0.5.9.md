# Bit Browser 0.5.9: media stops when you close the browser

**Fixed: videos and music kept playing after you closed Bit Browser.** "Keep playing in the background" kept media going (with a play/pause notification) even after you swiped Bit Browser away.

Now:
- **Close Bit Browser** (swipe it away from recent apps, or press Back to leave it) and every video and sound stops, and the media notification goes away.
- **Switch to another app or turn the screen off** and media still keeps playing, like before. You can turn that off too in Settings → Privacy protections → **Keep playing in the background**.
