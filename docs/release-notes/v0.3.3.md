# Bit Browser 0.3.3: sign-ins really stay now

**Found and fixed the real reason Bit Browser kept signing you out of Google and everything else on Android.**

On Android, every time Bit Browser opened a **PDF** (like a Shippo label) or a **private tab**, the web engine erased the saved sign-ins for every website. That is how the Android web view's "incognito" switch works, and Bit Browser used it for both. Each label you opened signed you out of Google.

- PDFs no longer touch your sign-ins.
- Private tabs now get their own separate cookie jar, so they can't wipe your normal sign-ins. What you do in private tabs is forgotten when you close the last private tab, use the panic button, or restart the app.
- If your phone's Android System WebView is too old to keep private tabs separate, Bit Browser tells you the first time you open a private tab. Your normal sign-ins are still never wiped. Updating Android System WebView from the Play Store fixes this.

After updating, sign in to Google one more time. It should stay that way.
