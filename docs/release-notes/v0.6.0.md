# Bit Browser 0.6.0: new logo, the Bit Globe

**Bit Browser has a new logo:** a detailed 128-pixel teal globe with an orange ring and an orange **B**. It's on:
- the phone's app icon (including Android's round icons and the one-colour "themed" icon)
- the splash screen
- the Windows, Mac and Linux app icons
- the logo at the top of the browser, in Settings, the Library and the new tab page

The logo at the top left still shows when you're in a private tab or when Bitter is driving: the line under it turns purple or pink.

Also in this update (from 0.5.9): **media stops when you close Bit Browser.**

## Download

| You have | Download |
|---|---|
| **Android** | `BitBrowser-0.6.0-android.apk` (install it over the old one) |
| **Windows** | `BitBrowser-Setup-0.6.0-x64.exe`, or let Bit Browser update itself |
| **Mac** | `BitBrowser-0.6.0-universal.dmg` |
| **Linux** | `BitBrowser-0.6.0-x86_64.AppImage` or `.deb` |

Your phone may keep showing the old icon until you restart it, because launchers cache icons.
