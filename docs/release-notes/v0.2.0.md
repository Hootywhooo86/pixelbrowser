# Bit Browser 0.2.0

Pixelbrowser is now **Bit Browser**, and its AI assistant is called **Bitter**.
This release fixes the phone layout and adds Brave-style tabs and settings, a
password manager, default-browser support, downloads and full-screen video.

## Download

| You have | Download |
|---|---|
| **Windows** | `BitBrowser-Setup-0.2.0-x64.exe` (most PCs), or `-arm64.exe` for ARM laptops |
| **Mac** (Intel or Apple Silicon) | `BitBrowser-0.2.0-universal.dmg` |
| **Linux** | `BitBrowser-0.2.0-x86_64.AppImage`, or the `.deb` for Ubuntu/Debian |
| **Android** | `BitBrowser-0.2.0-android.apk` |

The `.aab`, `.zip`, `.blockmap` and `latest*.yml` files are for Google Play and auto-updates. You don't need to download them.

## New name, same browser
- New pixel **B** logo, same colors.
- The AI assistant is now **Bitter**: "Ask Bitter about…", the Bitter sidebar and the Bitter panel on your phone.
- Your settings, AI keys, extensions and sync carry over. Installed desktop apps update to Bit Browser on their own.

## Phone
- **Fixed the top and bottom bars.** Nothing sits under the status bar or the gesture bar any more, on any screen. Buttons and links at the very bottom of a page can be tapped.
- **Bars get out of the way.** Scroll down and the address bar and buttons slide away. Scroll up, or tap the strip at the top, to bring them back.
- **Labeled buttons:** Back, Forward, Reload, Tabs, Bitter and Menu. Long-press any of them for a hint.
- **Brave-style tabs:** a grid of tab cards with page previews, search, normal and private tabs, swipe a card to close it, a big + button, and Close all.
- **Settings rebuilt** like Brave's, with search: Shields & privacy, Bitter AI, Sync, 128bit family, Search engines, Homepage, Downloads, Default browser, Media, Text size, Bit Password Manager, Payment methods, Addresses, and About.
- **Make Bit Browser your default browser.** Links from other apps open as new tabs.
- **Bit Password Manager:** offers to save passwords when you log in and fills them in next time. They're encrypted on your phone, and viewing them needs your fingerprint, face or PIN. Syncing them to your other devices (end-to-end encrypted) is off unless you turn it on.
- **Downloads work**, including APKs from GitHub. Find them under Menu → Downloads.
- **Full-screen video:** videos go full screen and turn sideways, and the bars hide until you exit.
- **Let Bitter take over** (Menu): a pink border and a live list of steps show while Bitter drives the page, with a big STOP button. It still asks before buying, sending or logging in.
- **128bit family** section in Settings. Accounts are coming soon. Everything there is off until you connect.

## Desktop
- Full-screen video hides the tabs, toolbar and sidebar, and puts them back when you exit.
- Settings → Default browser has a "Make Bit Browser your default browser" button.

## One-time effects of the rename
- **Windows:** the update installs over the old app in the same folder. The Start menu and desktop shortcuts are renamed "Bit Browser". If you pinned Pixelbrowser to the taskbar, pin Bit Browser again.
- **Mac:** download the new `.dmg` by hand. Mac builds aren't signed yet, so they can't auto-update. The app is now "Bit Browser" in Applications, so you can delete the old "Pixelbrowser" app.
- **Android:** install the APK over the old one. It's the same app, so nothing is lost.

## Still a preview
These builds aren't code-signed yet, so the same warnings as 0.1.0 apply:
- **Windows:** SmartScreen → **More info → Run anyway**.
- **Mac:** right-click → **Open**.
- **Android:** allow "Install unknown apps".
