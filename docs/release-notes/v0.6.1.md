# Bit Browser 0.6.1: cleaner, safer links

## Dangerous-site warnings, stronger (phone)
- A full red **DANGEROUS SITE AHEAD** screen now replaces the small pop-up, with one big **GET ME OUT OF HERE** button. "Open anyway" is under Details.
- It now also checks addresses you type, links from other apps, tabs reopened from last time, and downloads. Before, it only checked links you tapped on a page.
- The list still downloads to your phone and is checked there, so the sites you visit are never sent anywhere.

## Clean links (phone and computer)
- Tracking tags such as `utm_…`, `fbclid` and `gclid` are removed from links before the page opens.
- Google AMP copies open the publisher's real page instead.
- Redirect trackers (google.com/url, Facebook's l.php and others) are skipped, so you go straight to the site.
- Sign-in and payment pages are never changed. Where Shields are down for a site, its links aren't cleaned.
- Turn it off in Settings → Shields & privacy → Clean links.

## Forget a site when you close it (phone)
- Menu → **This site** → **Forget this site when I close it.** When the site's last tab closes, you're signed out and its cookies and saved data are deleted. Every other site stays signed in.
- It also happens the next time you open Bit Browser if you swiped the app away with the site still open.
- **Clear this site's cookies now** does it right away.
- See and undo the list in Settings → Shields & privacy → Forget when closed.

## Hide part of a page (phone)
- Menu → **Hide part of this page**, then tap the annoying banner, pop-up or box. Tap **WIDER** to grab the box around it, then **HIDE IT**.
- It stays hidden every time you visit that site. Undo it in Menu → This site → Hidden on this site.

## Add to home screen (phone)
- Menu → **Add to home screen** puts the site on your home screen with its own pixel icon, made from the site's icon.
- Tapping the icon goes back to the site's tab if it's already open, so you don't end up with a pile of copies.

## QR codes (phone and computer)
- Menu → **QR code for this page** shows a code another phone can scan to open the page. Copy or share the link from there too. On the computer it's in the menu and the right-click menu.
- Menu → **Scan QR code** opens the camera. Links open in a new tab, after the dangerous-site check.
