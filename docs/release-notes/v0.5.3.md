# Bit Browser 0.5.3: calmer pull to refresh

**Pull down to refresh is much less sensitive.** A page only reloads when you mean it:
- **Pull further:** about a quarter of the screen, roughly double before.
- **Hold for a moment:** the arrow turns orange when it's ready. Letting go early does nothing.
- **Pull straight down:** sideways swipes don't count.
- **Start at the top of the page:** a flick that just reached the top doesn't count, and neither do drags inside maps, chat boxes or scrolling lists.

Prefer no pull to refresh at all? Turn it off in Settings → Tabs and tab groups → **Pull down to refresh**.

Also includes 0.5.2's fix for Shippo's "Download label".
