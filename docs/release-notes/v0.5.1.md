# Bit Browser 0.5.1: labels download, whatever the site does

**A safety net for "Download label" buttons** (Shippo, insurance sites, invoices).

Sites hand over PDFs in many different ways. Bit Browser already handled the common ones: links, pop-ups, page-made files and files that open in a new window. Some sites do something else, like showing the PDF in a hidden preview, or fetching it and saving it through their own code. On the phone that ended in nothing happening.

Now, if a page makes or loads a PDF right after you tap something, and no other route saved it, Bit Browser saves it and opens it in the PDF viewer. You can print, save or share it from there. Tested against 14 different ways sites deliver PDFs, with no duplicates.

**New: download log.** Menu → Downloads now has a DOWNLOAD LOG (SHOW / SHARE). If a download still doesn't happen, tap SHARE and send it to me. It shows what the site did when you tapped, so it can be fixed. It's kept only on your phone and only leaves when you share it.
