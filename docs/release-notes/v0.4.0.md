# Bit Browser 0.4.0: the everyday update

Everything people reach for every day, on your phone and your computer.

## Browsing
- **Bookmarks:** save any page (Menu → Bookmark this page, or Ctrl+D / the star on the computer), sort them into folders, and find them in Bookmarks. They sync between your phone and computer. The computer also has a bookmarks bar.
- **History:** search everything you've visited, delete single pages, or clear the last hour, today or everything.
- **Reopen closed tab:** Menu → Reopen closed tab (Ctrl+Shift+T on the computer).
- **Find in page:** Menu → Find in page (Ctrl+F).
- **New tab page:** pixel shortcuts to your favourite sites (Shippo is there already), your pinned and synced tabs, the blocked count, your XP and this week's quests.
- **Reader mode:** a clean, pixel-styled view of articles with adjustable text.
- **Translate page:** with Bitter, or Google Translate when AI is off. Sites can be set to always translate.
- **Save page as PDF** and **Print page**, good for receipts and confirmations.
- **Pull down to refresh** (phone) and a **Desktop site** switch per site.
- **Picture-in-picture** for videos on the computer, and audio keeps playing when the window is minimized.

## Sites can use your camera, microphone and location if you let them
Video calls, maps and other sites that need these now work. Bit Browser asks first: Allow once, Always allow, or Block. Change it any time from Menu → This site, or review every site in Settings → Site settings.

## For shipping and business
- **Fill business details:** save your company name, address, phone and email once, then fill shipping forms in one tap (Menu → Fill business details).
- **Print labels right away:** PDF labels and invoices go straight to the print screen as soon as they download.
- **Save downloads to a folder you choose,** like your phone's Downloads.

## Passwords on the computer
The computer now has the Bit Password Manager too: it offers to save logins, fills them in (including Google's two-step sign-in), and keeps them encrypted. If you turn on password sync, the phone and computer share them end-to-end encrypted.

**Fixed:** passwords, addresses and cards didn't actually sync between devices before, even with sync turned on. They do now, still only if you turn it on.

## 128bit quests
Three new quests every week, the same on all your devices. For example: block 1,000 trackers, read 3 articles in reader mode, bookmark 5 sites. Each one finished earns XP. See them on the new tab page and in Settings → 128bit family.

## Crash reports (opt-in)
If you turn this on in Settings, Bit Browser keeps a note when something breaks so you can share it. It's off by default, stays on your device, and nothing is sent automatically.
