## Bit Shields: ad and tracker blocking
- Blocks ads, trackers and YouTube ads, using the same filter lists as uBlock Origin: EasyList, EasyPrivacy, uBlock filters, Peter Lowe's list, plus optional annoyances, cookie banners and regional lists.
- A **shield in the address bar** shows how many things were blocked on the page. Tap it to turn Shields down for just that site.
- Lists download straight from the people who make them and update every day. What you browse never leaves your device.
- Ads and trackers are often half of a page, so pages also load faster and use less battery.

## Smooth like silk
- Only your current tab and the two before it stay running. Older tabs sleep and pick up where you left off.
- Videos and sound in hidden tabs pause.
- Pages no longer resize or jump while you scroll. "Hide bars when scrolling" is now a setting, off by default.
- Tabs only redraw when they change, and the Tabs screen opens instantly.
- Autoplay setting: off, muted only (the default) or on.
- Computer: smooth scrolling, and less redrawing while pages load or the window resizes.

## Looks
- **Pixel themes:** Bit, Game Boy, Arcade, Sunset, Mono (high contrast) and Light, or build your own.
- Chunky or classic tab cards, retro sound effects (off by default), and the new Bitter mascot.
- Text, buttons and spacing lined up and cleaned up on every screen.
- **Tablets and Chromebooks:** a tab strip along the top, Bitter as a side panel, a wider tab grid, two-pane settings, and the screen can turn.

## Bitter
- **Summarize this page**, and **Summarize this video** on YouTube (uses the video's captions).
- **Explain with Bitter:** highlight text and pick it from the menu (right-click on the computer).
- **Sources:** answers show where each fact came from. Tap a source to open it.
- **Spending cap** per day or month, so Bitter can't run up your AI bill.
- **Activity log** of everything Bitter clicked, typed or opened.
- **Spaces:** Personal, Work and 128bit, each with its own tabs and its own Bitter memory. Make your own too.

## Privacy and security
- **Panic button:** closes private tabs and wipes recent history (Menu, long-press the B logo, or Ctrl+Shift+X).
- **Lock private tabs** with your fingerprint or face (a PIN on the computer).
- **Phishing and malware warnings**, checked on your device against free blocklists.
- **Secure DNS** on the computer (Cloudflare, Quad9, NextDNS or your own). The phone shows where Android's Private DNS setting is.
- **Your data:** a page showing what's stored on the device, what syncs, and buttons to delete it.

## 128bit family
- **XP, levels, streaks and badges**, like "1,000 trackers squashed". They're kept on your devices and synced between them.
- Phone numbers open in **BitCaller**, and IMDb/TMDB pages have **Add to 128bittracker**.

## Good to know
- YouTube changes how it serves ads often. When an ad slips through, the lists usually catch up within a day or two.
- On iPhone, Shields hides ads and blocks known ad sites, but can't block every hidden request yet.
- Google Play doesn't allow apps that block YouTube ads. The APK from this page is fine, but a Play Store version will need that part switched off.
