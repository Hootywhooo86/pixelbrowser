# Bit Browser 0.3.4: pinned tabs, and your tabs come back

## Pin tabs
- **Phone:** in the Tabs screen, tap the pin button on a card or long-press it. Or open a page and use Menu → **Pin this tab**.
- **Computer:** right-click a tab → **Pin tab**.
- Pinned tabs sit at the front with an orange pin. They can't be closed by accident: swiping, X, Ctrl+W and "Close all" skip them. Unpin to close one.
- Pinned tabs belong to their Space, so your Work tabs stay in Work.

## Your tabs come back
- When you reopen Bit Browser, your tabs are where you left them, in each Space (Personal, Work, 128bit), with the same tab selected.
- Only the tab you're on loads at first, and the rest load when you open them, so startup stays fast.
- Don't want that? Phone: Settings → Tabs and tab groups → Reopen my tabs. Computer: Settings → Spaces → On startup. Pinned tabs always come back either way.
- Private tabs are never saved.
