# Bit Browser 0.4.3: "Sign in with Google" works

**Sign-in buttons that open a pop-up now work**, like "Continue with Google" on claude.ai, and "Sign in with Apple / Microsoft / GitHub" on other sites.

These buttons open a small pop-up window. You sign in there, it tells the page you're signed in, and it closes. Bit Browser didn't allow pop-ups, so the sign-in page opened as a normal page with nothing to report back to, and you were left on a blank `accounts.google.com/gsi/transform` page.

- **Phone:** sign-in pop-ups open full screen over the page with a **CLOSE** button, then close on their own when you're signed in. Back works inside them. Ordinary "open in new window" links still open as a new tab.
- **Computer:** sign-in pop-ups open as a small window on top of Bit Browser. Other new-window links still open as tabs.
- claude.ai and anthropic.com were added to the sites allowed to share sign-in cookies with Google during sign-in.
