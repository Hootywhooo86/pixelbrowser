# Bit Browser 0.3.2: stay signed in

You should no longer have to sign in to Google and other sites every time you open Bit Browser.

- **Sign-ins are saved right away.** Your login is written to storage as soon as a page loads and whenever you leave the app, so closing it right after signing in no longer loses it.
- **Google treats Bit Browser like a normal browser.** Bit Browser used to identify itself as an app with a built-in web view (on the phone) or as Electron (on the computer), and Google then shortened or refused "stay signed in". It now identifies itself like Chrome.
- **Google, YouTube, Gmail, Microsoft, Apple, Amazon, Facebook, GitHub and Shippo** can share their sign-in between their own sites (for example, signing in to Google also signs you in to YouTube). Every other site still has third-party cookies blocked.
- **Two-step sign-ins work with the password manager.** On sites like Google that ask for your email first and your password on the next screen, Bit Browser now remembers both, offers to save them, and offers to fill both steps next time.
